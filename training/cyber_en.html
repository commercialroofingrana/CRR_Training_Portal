🏢 Commercial Roofing Rana LLC  |  Rev. 1.0  |  Effective April 27, 2025  |  Annual Requirement

📋 About This Program

🎯

Purpose

Establish minimum expectations for cyber and information security at Commercial Roofing Rana LLC across all work activities.

👷

Who It Covers

All CRR employees, supervisors, temporary workers, and subcontractors on jobsites, facilities, shops, yards, or traveling for company business.

📅

When Training Is Required

Before covered tasks, when job duties change, after a new hazard is introduced, after an incident or near miss, and whenever retraining is needed.

⚖️

Stricter Rule Applies

Customer, general contractor, site-specific, OSHA, state, and local requirements may be stricter than this program. The stricter requirement always governs.

📌 Training may include classroom instruction, toolbox talks, hands-on demonstrations, manufacturer instructions, and jobsite coaching.

👥 Roles & Responsibilities

🏢

Management

Provide resources, training, equipment, enforcement, and oversight necessary to implement this program.

🦺

Supervisors / Foremen

Plan the work, identify hazards, verify controls are in place, conduct jobsite briefings, stop unsafe work, and document inspections or incidents.

👷

Employees

Follow this program, use assigned equipment correctly, report hazards immediately, and stop work when conditions are unsafe.

🤝

Subcontractors

Comply with CRR requirements, customer/site requirements, OSHA requirements, and provide training or qualification documentation when requested.

✋

Stop Work Authority

All employees have the authority and responsibility to stop work if they believe a condition or activity could result in injury, property damage, environmental impact, or a violation of customer or regulatory requirements.

⚠️ Work shall NOT resume until the hazard is corrected or controlled. This applies to cyber and data security risks just as it does to physical hazards.

🛡️ What This Program Protects

Information security covers all data, systems, and records used in company operations

👤

Customer Info

Names, addresses, project details, and portal credentials

📊

Company Data

Estimates, contracts, photos, financials, and business records

👷

Employee Info

HR records, pay information, personal data, and credentials

💻

Devices & Systems

Computers, phones, tablets, CRM, accounting, and project software

☁️

Cloud & Email

Cloud storage, email accounts, customer portals, and file sharing

🖨️

Printed Documents

Physical documents, drawings, proposals, and printed contracts

🔐

🔑 User Access & Passwords

✅

Access Only What You Need

Employees may access only the systems and information required for their specific job duties

🚫

Never Share Passwords

Passwords shall not be shared with coworkers, family members, or anyone — including management

🔒

Keep Passwords Separate

Do not reuse business passwords for personal accounts when avoidable. Never store passwords in plain view

👁️

Lock When You Leave

All devices must be locked when unattended — password, PIN, biometric, or equivalent required

🚪

Access Removed on Departure

Access shall be removed or changed immediately when an employee leaves or changes roles

📱

Use MFA

Multi-factor authentication required for email, banking, CRM, cloud storage & customer portals where available

🎣

🎣 Email, Phishing & Links

Phishing attacks target roofing companies — stay alert to fake invoices, wire fraud, and impersonated vendors

🚫

Don't Open Suspicious Attachments

Unexpected files, invoices, or links — even from known contacts — may be malicious

📞

Verify Payment Changes by Phone

Wire instructions, vendor banking updates, unusual requests — always call a known trusted number to confirm

📧

Don't Forward Sensitive Info to Personal Email

Customer data, proposals, financials, and contracts must stay in approved company systems

🚨

Report It Immediately

Suspicious emails, texts, or unusual account activity must be reported to management right away

🔗

Hover Before You Click

Check where a link actually goes before clicking — fraudulent links often look legitimate

🧠

When in Doubt, Don't

If something feels off about an email or request, stop and verify before acting. It's always okay to ask

💻 Devices & Data Handling

🔐

Lock All Devices When Unattended

Password, PIN, or biometric lock required on all company computers, phones, and tablets

🚨

Report Lost or Stolen Devices Immediately

Every minute matters — immediate reporting allows accounts to be secured and data protected

📁

Store Data in Approved Systems

Customer documents, drawings, photos, proposals, and contracts go in company-approved platforms — not personal drives

🖨️

Secure Printed Documents

Physical documents with sensitive information must be secured while in use and properly disposed of when done

🌐

Use Trusted Networks

Avoid accessing company systems on public Wi-Fi without a VPN or equivalent protection

🚨 Incident Response

⚡ If you suspect a breach, compromise, or lost device — notify management IMMEDIATELY. Time is critical.
1

Stop & Contain

Disconnect the affected device if safe to do so. Don't delete anything — preserve evidence.

2

Notify Management

Report any suspected data breach, account compromise, or lost/stolen device to management right away.

3

Secure Accounts

Management will reset credentials, change access permissions, and lock compromised accounts.

4

Notify Affected Parties

Management evaluates whether customers, vendors, or employees need to be notified per applicable requirements.

5

Document Everything

Record what happened, when it was discovered, what was affected, and what corrective actions were taken.

6

Review & Prevent

Identify the root cause and update procedures to prevent recurrence. Provide retraining if needed.
